## 2. SEI byte layout — confirmed byte-for-byte
Our SEI for `frame_id = 1`, **de-emulation-prevented** RBSP:
```
05 14 53 6e 69 70 65 49 74 46 72 6d 49 44 00 00 00 01  00 00 00 01  80
│  │  └──────────────── 16-byte UUID ───────────────┘  └ id BE ─┘   └ rbsp_stop (0x80)
│  └ payloadSize = 0x14 = 20
└ payloadType = 5  (user_data_unregistered)
```
On-wire (with start code + NAL header + emulation-prevention) the same NAL is:
```
00 00 00 01 4e 01 05 14 53 6e 69 70 65 49 74 46 72 6d 49 44 00 00 03 00 01 00 00 03 00 01 80
└ start ──┘ └hdr┘                                    ^^EPB^^          ^^EPB^^
```
Answering your sub-questions:
- **(a) payloadSize** uses standard SEI `ff…`/last-byte encoding. Here it's a
  single `0x14` (20 < 255). Same for payloadType.
- **(b)** The 16-byte UUID **immediately** precedes the 4 frame_id bytes —
  nothing in between.
- **(c)** Exactly **one** trailing byte after the id: `0x80` (`rbsp_stop_one_bit`
  + alignment). Then the NAL ends.
- **Emulation-prevention is present** (you already de-EPB): the UUID tail
  `…44 00 00 00 01` and the id `00 00 00 01` each pick up an inserted `03` →
  `…44 00 00 03 00 01` and `00 00 03 00 01`. De-EPB first, then read.
- **frame_id is uint32 big-endian.**

## 3. ⚠️ Three stream facts your parser must handle
1. **x265 emits its OWN `user_data_unregistered` SEI** (also NAL type 39,
   payloadType 5) carrying its version/options string, UUID
   `ff ff ff ff ff ff ff ff 12 2c a2 de …`. **You MUST filter by our UUID**
   (`53 6e 69 70 65 49 74 46 72 6d 49 44 00 00 00 01`). Matching only
   "type 39 + payloadType 5" will also match x265's SEI and you'd decode its
   ASCII as a frame_id. (x265's appears ~once near stream start; ours is exactly
   one per AU.)
2. **No AUD (access-unit delimiter) NALs** are emitted (`x265 no-aud`). If your
   AU segmentation relies on AUD, tell us — we can add `aud=true` on `h265parse`
   trivially. Otherwise segment on the first VCL NAL (types 0–31).
3. **Headers placement differs between the file and the live RTP stream.** In
   the **elementary stream / sample file**, VPS/SPS/PPS appear **once at the
   very start** (`x265 no-repeat-headers`). On the **live RTP path**,
   `rtph265pay config-interval=1` re-inserts VPS/SPS/PPS **before each IDR**, so
   you can join mid-stream. Sample NAL histogram: VPS×1, SPS×1, PPS×1, prefix-
   SEI×43 (42 ours + 1 x265), IDR×1, CRA×1, TRAIL_R×40.
